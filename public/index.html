How to export from your iPhone
  1. Open the Health app and tap your picture or initials at the top right.
  2. Tap Export All Health Data, then Export. This can take a few minutes.
  3. Save the file (export.zip) to Files or send it to your computer, then choose it below.

The file is unpacked on this device. Only the summaries below are saved, encrypted. The zip itself is not uploaded.
